This Data Processing Agreement (this "**DPA**") forms part of the Agreement
between **[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** ("**Processor**", "we", "Sentinel") and

If there is a conflict between this DPA and the Agreement, this DPA controls
with respect to Processing of Personal Data.

## 1. Definitions

Terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Agreement or in
applicable Data Protection Law. "**Data Protection Law**" means all laws
applicable to the Processing of Personal Data under this DPA, which may
include the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended
(CCPA/CPRA), and other US state privacy laws, **as applicable to the parties
and the data at issue** — the specific laws in scope are listed at
`[GOVERNING DATA PROTECTION LAWS]`.

- "**Controller**", "**Processor**", "**Data Subject**", "**Personal Data**",
  "**Processing**", and "**Personal Data Breach**" have the meanings given in
  the GDPR (or the equivalent concepts under other applicable Data Protection
  Law, e.g. "Business" / "Service Provider" under the CCPA/CPRA).
- "**Sub-processor**" means any third party engaged by Processor to Process
  Personal Data under this DPA.

## 2. Roles of the parties

For Personal Data submitted to or generated by the Service, **Customer is the
Controller** and **Sentinel is the Processor**. Customer determines the
purposes and means of Processing; Sentinel Processes Personal Data only on
Customer's documented instructions, as set out in Section 3 and Annex 1.

The Agreement, this DPA, and Customer's configuration and use of the Service
constitute Customer's complete and final documented instructions.

## 3. Scope and purpose of Processing

Sentinel Processes Personal Data only to provide the Service: to receive an
approval request from Customer's AI agent, notify a human approver, capture
the approver's decision, record a tamper-evident audit trail of the
transaction, deliver result webhooks to Customer, and operate, secure, and
support the Service. The subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose of
Processing, and the categories of Data Subjects and Personal Data, are
described in **Annex 1**.

Sentinel will not Process Personal Data for any other purpose, will not "sell"
or "share" Personal Data as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and
will not retain, use, or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business
relationship with Customer or as otherwise permitted by Data Protection Law.

## 5. Security measures

Sentinel implements and maintains the technical and organizational measures
described in **Annex 3**, designed to protect Personal Data against
unauthorized or unlawful Processing and against accidental loss, destruction,
or damage. Customer acknowledges that the measures in Annex 3 reflect the
Service as currently implemented and that security is a shared
responsibility — Customer is responsible for the items in Section 6.

## 6. Customer responsibilities

Customer is responsible for: configuring the Service appropriately;
safeguarding its API keys (which grant full control of its tenant);
**obtaining all consents and providing all notices required to send approval
requests to its chosen approvers**, including any consent required to contact
approvers by SMS or email (e.g. TCPA consent for US phone numbers); ensuring
it has a lawful basis to submit Personal Data; verifying inbound webhook
signatures; and not submitting special-category data or data subject to
sector-specific regimes (e.g. PHI under HIPAA, cardholder data under PCI DSS)
unless expressly agreed in writing at `[SPECIAL DATA ADDENDUM REFERENCE]`.

## 7. Data Subject rights

Taking into account the nature of the Processing, Sentinel will assist
Customer by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as
possible, in fulfilling Customer's obligation to respond to Data Subject
requests to exercise rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction,
portability, and objection.

Because Sentinel acts as Processor, if Sentinel receives a Data Subject
request directly, it will, unless legally prohibited, promptly forward the
request to Customer and will not respond except on Customer's instructions.
Customer can action most requests directly through the Service's tenant-scoped
APIs and `[DASHBOARD/SUPPORT CHANNEL]`.

## 8. Personal Data Breach notification

Sentinel will notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within
`[N — e.g. 72]` hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach
affecting Customer's Personal Data. The notification will describe, to the
extent known: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number
of Data Subjects and records affected, the likely consequences, and the
measures taken or proposed. Sentinel will cooperate reasonably with Customer's
own breach-response and regulator/Data-Subject notification obligations.
Sentinel's notification is not an acknowledgment of fault or liability.

## 9. Deletion and return

Upon termination or expiry of the Agreement, and on Customer's request,
Sentinel will delete or return Customer's Personal Data within `[N]` days,
except to the extent retention is required by applicable law. Note: the audit
trail is designed to be tamper-evident and append-only (see Annex 3); deletion
of audit records is performed at the tenant level on termination and is
described at `[DELETION PROCEDURE REFERENCE]`. Backups are purged on the
rolling backup cycle described at `[BACKUP RETENTION REFERENCE]`.

## Annex 3 — Technical and organizational security measures

> These statements describe controls **as implemented in the Service today**,
> derived from the engineering threat model and architecture docs. They make
> **no** certification claims. Counsel and security should verify each line
> before this annex is shared externally.

**Access control and tenant isolation**
- Every authenticated API endpoint is scoped to a single tenant; one tenant's
  API key cannot read or modify another tenant's data.
- API keys are random secrets stored **only as a SHA-256 hash**; the raw value
  is shown to the customer once and is not recoverable from our systems.
  Privileged admin endpoints require a separate admin credential.

**Encryption**
- All traffic is served over TLS (TLS 1.2+); HMAC comparisons use a
  constant-time comparison.
- Data at rest is encrypted (AES-256, provider-managed via the hosting
  platform).

**Approval and decision integrity**
- Approval magic-links are HMAC-SHA256 tokens bound to a single approval
  action, a single decision, and an expiry; they cannot be forged without the
  server signing secret and cannot be replayed (each decision token is
  recorded as single-use, and an already-decided approval rejects further
  decisions).
- Outbound webhooks are signed with a per-endpoint HMAC secret so customers
  can verify authenticity; inbound Stripe webhooks are signature-verified with
  a replay window.

**Audit trail (tamper-evidence)**
- Approval and execution events are written to an append-only,
  **hash-chained** audit log (each event hash incorporates the previous event
  hash), serialized per tenant so the chain is deterministic. A customer can
  verify their chain's integrity through a read-only verification endpoint.
- The Service includes **RFC 3161 trusted-timestamping capability** for audit
  events. *Accuracy note: this capability is implemented in code but is
  **activated only when a Time Stamping Authority endpoint is configured**;
  signature-chain (X.509) validation of TSA tokens is not yet performed
  in-product. Do not represent timestamping as continuously active or as a
  certified service.*

**Data minimization and logging hygiene**
- Approver phone/email destinations are stored as a keyed HMAC hash plus a
  last-4 display value where full retention is not required for delivery;
  approval magic-link URLs carry no PII.
- Request and response bodies are not written to application logs.

**Rate limiting and abuse controls**
- A Redis-backed rate limiter throttles approval creation per IP and per
  domain. *Accuracy note: the limiter currently fails open if Redis is
  unavailable — disclose this honestly rather than overstating it.*

**Operational**
- Single production environment in a US region (us-east); no multi-region
  failover today.
- Dependency and vulnerability scanning, formal access-review cadence, and
  background checks are `[STATE WHAT IS ACTUALLY IN PLACE — do not assert
  controls that are only roadmapped]`.
